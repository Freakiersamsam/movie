🎬 about cinemdle

your daily cinema challenge

Welcome to Cinemdle, the daily movie guessing game for true cinema lovers! Inspired by Wordle but designed for film buffs, Cinemdle challenges you to identify movies from iconic quotes, actors, and clues.

how it works

Every day, you get 5 new movies to guess, each with increasing difficulty:

  • Difficulty 1: Extremely popular blockbusters
  • Difficulty 2: Well-known classics
  • Difficulty 3: Cult favorites & film buff picks
  • Difficulty 4: Deeper cuts & indie darlings
  • Difficulty 5: Arthouse & international cinema

For each movie, you get up to 6 progressive hints:

  1. First quote from the movie
  2. Second quote (consecutive dialogue)
  3. Actor who spoke the first quote
  4. Actor who spoke the second quote
  5. Release year
  6. Movie title (revealed)

Guess correctly with fewer hints to improve your stats!

the movie database

Our carefully curated database spans cinema history, from Golden Age classics to modern blockbusters, indie gems to international masterpieces. Each movie is selected for its cultural impact and recognizability within its difficulty tier.

All quotes are consecutive dialogue from actual film scripts, ensuring authenticity and accuracy.
