how to play

guess the movie from quotes and clues. you get 6 hints per round.

  • hint 1-2: two quotes from the movie
  • hint 3-4: actors who spoke those quotes
  • hint 5: release year
  • hint 6: the movie title

play 5 rounds daily (difficulty 1-5). come back tomorrow for new movies!

keyboard shortcuts

  • enter - submit guess
  • ctrl+h - reveal next hint
  • ctrl+s - show stats
  • ? - show this help
  • ↑ / ↓ - navigate autocomplete
