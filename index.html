How to Play Cinemdle - Daily Movie Quote Game

Cinemdle is a free daily movie guessing game where you identify films from quotes and clues. Test your cinema knowledge with 5 rounds of increasing difficulty each day.

Game Rules

Each day features 5 new movie puzzles with progressive difficulty levels:

  • Round 1: Popular blockbusters and mainstream movies
  • Round 2: Well-known classics and fan favorites
  • Round 3: Cult films and cinema buff picks
  • Round 4: Indie movies and deeper cuts
  • Round 5: Arthouse and international cinema

How Hints Work

You receive up to 6 progressive hints per movie:

  1. First iconic quote from the film
  2. Second consecutive quote (dialogue continues)
  3. Actor who spoke the first quote
  4. Actor who spoke the second quote
  5. Release year of the movie
  6. Movie title revealed

Features

  • 289 movies in curated database spanning classic to modern cinema
  • Free to play with no registration required
  • Track your statistics, win rate, and winning streaks
  • Share results with friends on social media
  • Keyboard shortcuts for power users
  • Mobile-friendly responsive design
  • Daily reset at midnight for fresh challenges

Tips for Success

Master the movie quote game with these strategies:

  • Read quotes carefully for context clues about genre and era
  • Use the autocomplete feature to explore movie titles
  • Remember that difficulty increases with each round
  • Come back daily to maintain your winning streak
  • Share your results to compete with friends
