How to Play

Guess the movie from dialogue quotes!

  • You get 7 progressive hints
  • Each hint costs one guess attempt
  • The fewer hints you need, the better your score
  • A new movie every day at midnight
  • Try to maintain your streak!

Hints revealed in order:

  1. First quote
  2. Second quote
  3. Third quote
  4. Actor who said quote 1
  5. Actor who said quote 2
  6. Year released
  7. Movie title (answer)
