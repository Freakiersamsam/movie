Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 29, 2025

Introduction

Welcome to Cinemdle. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our website.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

Cinemdle does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to play the game.

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect:

  • Usage Data: Game statistics, guess patterns, completion rates
  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, screen resolution
  • Analytics Data: Pages visited, time spent, interactions (via Google Analytics)
  • Cookies: Small data files stored on your device (see Cookies section)

Local Storage

Cinemdle uses your browser's local storage to save:

  • Your game progress and statistics
  • Your daily streak
  • Developer mode preferences

This data is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

Cookies and Tracking

Essential Cookies

We use local storage (similar to cookies) to remember your game state. These are necessary for the game to function.

Analytics Cookies

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. This helps us improve the game experience. Google Analytics collects:

  • Pages you visit
  • How long you spend on each page
  • Your approximate location (city/country level)
  • Your device and browser type

You can opt-out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Advertising Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners may use cookies to:

  • Show you relevant ads based on your interests
  • Measure ad performance
  • Prevent the same ad from showing too many times

You can customize your ad preferences at Google Ad Settings.

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the game service
  • Improve game features and user experience
  • Analyze usage patterns and trends
  • Display relevant advertisements
  • Prevent fraud and abuse

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Google Analytics: For usage analytics
  • Google AdSense: For advertising services
  • Cloudflare: Our hosting provider (for website delivery and security)

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we have about you
  • Delete your local game data (clear browser storage)
  • Opt-out of analytics tracking
  • Customize ad preferences

Children's Privacy

Cinemdle is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at:

Email: privacy@cinemdle.com
